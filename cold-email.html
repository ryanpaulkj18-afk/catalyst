The infrastructure

Six layers between you and the inbox.

Deliverability isn’t a setting — it’s an architecture. Here’s every piece we build and own on your behalf.

01

Isolated Domains

Sending is spread across twenty separate domains, fully insulated from your primary brand domain. One never drags down the others — and your root domain stays pristine.

02

Warmed Inboxes

Sixty inboxes, gradually warmed to build genuine sending reputation before a single prospect is touched. Volume scales without ever tripping a provider’s spam threshold.

03

Full SPF / DKIM / DMARC

Every domain is authenticated end to end. Mailbox providers can verify exactly who you are, which is the difference between the primary tab and the spam folder.

04

Intent-Based List Building

We don’t spray a bought list. Prospects are sourced on live buying signals — hiring, funding, tech moves — so you reach people already primed to act.

05

Claude Personalization

Every message is personalized at scale through the Claude API — researched, specific, and human. No “Hi {FirstName}” templates that get instantly deleted.

06

Agentic Reply Triage

An autonomous agent reads every reply, classifies intent, drafts a response, and pushes booking-ready leads to your calendar. Interested prospects never go cold.

How it works

From zero to live sends in four weeks.

No shortcuts on warmup — that patience is exactly why our campaigns keep landing while others burn out.

Week 1

Domain & Inbox Setup

We register and configure all twenty domains and sixty inboxes, wire up SPF, DKIM and DMARC, and lock down the technical foundation end to end.

Weeks 2–4

Warmup Period

Inboxes are warmed gradually to build authentic reputation with mailbox providers. Meanwhile we build your intent-based list and write your sequences.

Week 4+

Live Sends Begin

Personalized campaigns go live, reply triage switches on, and qualified meetings start landing on your calendar. You review wins — we run the engine.
