The practical bits

What you’ll want
to know first.

Do we have to change our existing software?

Not automatically. We check your calendar, CRM and phone setup first. We’ll confirm what can connect, what needs changing and what stays manual before agreeing the work.

Is this just an AI answering service?

No. Call handling can be part of the setup, alongside campaigns, booking and follow-up. We choose the parts that fit your business. An assistant follows approved instructions; your team handles technical advice, pricing and anything outside those instructions.

Can you work with the enquiries we already get?

Yes, we can scope a response and follow-up workflow around existing enquiries. If the gap is demand instead, we can discuss a campaign. You don’t have to add advertising just to improve handling.

What does it cost?

We scope the service before quoting. Any advertising spend and third-party software costs are made clear separately, so you can see what you’re committing to.

Can you promise a number of jobs?

No. Demand, service area, available capacity, pricing and how your team closes work all affect results. We agree what to measure and review the actual enquiry and booking journey.
