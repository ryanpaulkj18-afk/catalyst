What exactly is the system?

An AI-powered growth system with three parts: a growth engine that brings new homeowners to you, an AI booking agent that books them in, and a site built to convert. How they connect for your business is what we map out on the call.

How hands-off is it really?

After a short setup call and a quick review in the first week, it runs without you. We manage and improve it in the background. You get booked jobs in your diary and a short monthly update.

Will the AI booking agent put customers off?

You see exactly how it handles real enquiries before it goes live, and you approve what it says. Anything outside its instructions goes straight to you. If you’re not happy with it, it doesn’t go live.

Do I have to change my software?

No. The booking agent works with the calendar and tools you already use. We confirm what connects before anything is built.

Is the $15K–$40K guaranteed?

No, and anyone who guarantees a number without seeing your business is guessing. It’s the range the system is built and measured against. We model your likely figure on the call, then report against it every month.

What does it cost?

It depends on your area and how hard you want to grow, so a number here would be a guess. You get a clear price on the call, with any media spend shown separately. No long contract.
